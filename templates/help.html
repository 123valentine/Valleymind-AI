Frequently asked questions

How do I get started?

Open ValleyMind AI and create an account with your email or Google Sign-In. You can start chatting immediately — the assistant walks you through what it can do, or you can ask directly for an image, a video or a studio project.

What can the assistant actually do?

Chat and answer questions (with live web, news and sports where needed), remember facts about you, generate images, generate videos, run multi-persona Round Table discussions, and create or edit video in the Studio. Just describe what you want in plain language.

How do I log in or reset my password?

Use the login screen with your email and password or Google. Forgot your password? The reset flow asks a security question you set up on your first sign-in — answer it correctly to choose a new password.

Where are my generated images and videos stored?

Everything you create is saved to your personal media library in the app. You can view, search, download or delete your media from there at any time.

Why did my video take a long time (or fail)?

Video generation is the most compute-heavy feature and can take a few minutes. Generation is billed against a daily budget, so unusually heavy usage may be temporarily unavailable. Your completed videos are always saved to your gallery when they finish.

Does ValleyMind AI remember what I say?

Yes — long-term memory is a core feature. It remembers facts you share, project details and preferences so it can be helpful later. You can review and manage this memory in Settings at any time.

How do I report a bug or send feedback?

Inside the app, open the Suggestions tool and describe the issue or idea — it goes straight to the team (with an optional WhatsApp follow-up). You can also reach us on the Contact page.

Is my data private?

We take privacy seriously. See the Privacy Policy for exactly what we collect, how it's stored and the third-party AI services used to power the app.

Still stuck?

We're happy to help — reach out and we'll get back to you.
