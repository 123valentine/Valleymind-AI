Home/Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how ValleyMind AI ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, stores and protects your personal information when you use our website and app. By using ValleyMind AI, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you create an account we collect your email address, a password (stored only as a secure hash), and a security question of your choice. If you sign in with Google, we receive the name, email address and profile picture from your Google account.

Content you provide

This includes your chat messages, prompts, uploaded files (images, video, audio), generated media and any facts you share with the assistant. This content is used to deliver the service and to provide your assistant's long-term memory.

Usage information

We may collect basic technical data such as device type, browser, approximate region and timestamps to operate, protect and improve the service.

2. How we use your information

  • To provide, maintain and improve ValleyMind AI.
  • To power the assistant's long-term memory of your preferences.
  • To generate the images, videos and edits you request.
  • To respond to support requests and feedback.
  • To protect the security of accounts and the platform.

3. Third-party services

ValleyMind AI relies on trusted third-party providers to deliver features. Your prompts and media may be processed by these services to fulfill your requests:

  • AI model providers — Groq, OpenRouter, NVIDIA, Google Gemini and Alibaba Model Studio (image/video generation).
  • Search & data — TinyFish Search, DuckDuckGo, Wikipedia, Newscatcher and sports APIs for live answers.
  • Memory & storage — Pinecone (semantic memory), MongoDB (persistence), and Cloudflare R2 (media storage).
  • Authentication — Google Sign-In.
  • Speech — Groq Whisper for voice-to-text.

Each provider processes data only to the extent needed to serve your request and is subject to its own privacy and security terms. Where we store your media and chats ourselves, the data is kept securely and is only accessible to you through your account.

4. Cookies and local storage

We use cookies and browser local storage to keep you signed in and to remember app preferences (theme, fonts, settings). These are functional cookies — we do not use advertising trackers.

5. How long we keep data

We keep your account, chats and media for as long as your account is active so the assistant can remember your history. You can delete individual media items at any time, and you may request full deletion of your account and data by contacting us.

6. Children's privacy

ValleyMind AI is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

8. Security

We use HTTPS encryption, hashed passwords and restricted access to production systems. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be posted on this page with a revised date. Continued use of the service after changes means you accept the updated policy.

10. Contact us

Questions about this policy or your data? Contact us or email support@valleymind.ai.
