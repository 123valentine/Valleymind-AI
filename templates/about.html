What is ValleyMind AI?

ValleyMind AI started as a single idea: a personal AI assistant that actually gets to know you. Instead of treating every conversation as a blank slate, it builds long-term memory about the people, projects and preferences that matter to you — and uses that context to be genuinely useful over time.

Today it has grown into a complete creative suite. Beyond intelligent chat, ValleyMind AI can generate images and videos from a text prompt, run multi-persona Round Table conversations where several AI crew members debate and answer together, and edit video in the built-in Studio — complete with templates, assets, voiceovers and automated short-form editing.

Meet the crew

Conversations are powered by a small cast of AI personas, each with its own voice and personality:

  • Marcus — the founder's original assistant. Thoughtful, capable and the primary voice of the app.
  • Elena — creative, expressive and detail-oriented.
  • Angelina — sharp, energetic and direct.

All three share one long-term memory of you, so the assistant you talk to today remembers what you discussed yesterday — no matter which voice you choose.

What makes it different

  • Memory that matters — long-term facts, projects and preferences persist across sessions.
  • Media generation built in — images and videos generated straight from chat.
  • A real creative studio — edit, assemble and auto-edit video without leaving the app.
  • Reliable by design — a multi-provider AI engine keeps the assistant responsive even when one provider fails.
  • Mobile-first — works as an installable app on your phone or desktop.

The people behind it

ValleyMind AI is founded and built by Egbujie Valentine (K), an engineer who believes AI should be a daily companion — personal, creative and practical. The project continues to evolve in the open, with new capabilities arriving on a regular basis.
